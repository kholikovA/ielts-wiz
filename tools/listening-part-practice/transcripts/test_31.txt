[104.9] This trek is being specifically organised to help education in the Semira region.
[110.4] Last year we helped train teachers for the disabled and this year we're focusing on the pupils.

[154.8] Now, the Semira Mountains are among the highest in the world but you mustn't be too daunted.
[160.2] We will mainly be trekking in the foothills only although there will be spectacular views even in the foothills.

[170.0] If you aren't now and you're interested in coming with us,
[172.7] you have plenty of time to get into shape.

[297.8] Then you have a rest day, that's day five, before going to the spectacular Kumi [[18|temple]], with 12th-century carvings, set in a small forest by a lake, and that's day six, the highlight for many.
[311.7] We stay near there for day seven, because then comes the hardest day, walking through very mountainous country, but culminating in a swim in the Pate Falls.
[321.5] This is the highest [[19|waterfall]] in the region.

[324.8] Day nine is much easier, with part of the day spent in a [[20|village]] where they make some of the gorgeous red blankets, then back down to Kishba and the journey home.

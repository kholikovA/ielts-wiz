Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

IELTS Wiz (“IELTS Wiz”, “we”, “us”) provides online IELTS preparation at ielts-wiz.com and its subdomains, including student.ielts-wiz.com (together, the “Service”). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email hello@ielts-wiz.com.

1. Information we collect

Information from Google Sign-In

The only way to create an account is to sign in with Google. When you do, Google shares the following with us, and nothing more:

  • your name;
  • your email address and whether Google has verified it;
  • your profile picture URL;
  • your Google account identifier (a number that tells us it is the same Google account each time).

We request only the basic openid, email and profile scopes. We do not get access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data, and we never see your Google password.

Information you give us

  • Profile and onboarding answers, such as your target band score, study timeframe and reason for taking IELTS.
  • Practice content: your answers to Listening and Reading tests, Writing essays, vocabulary lists, flashcards and dictation attempts.
  • Class activity if a teacher adds you to a class: homework submissions, attendance and notes your teacher records.
  • Chat messages in the Student Hub: the text, photos, files and voice messages you send to your teachers or in your class and group chats. Everyone in that chat can see them, and the teacher who manages a chat can delete messages in it.
  • Messages you send us by email.

Information generated as you use the Service

  • Progress data: scores, band estimates, completion times, streaks and review history.
  • Sign-up context, recorded once when you first sign in: your time zone, browser language, device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), the referring website, any campaign (UTM) parameters in the link you arrived from, and the first page you visited.
  • Study activity in the Student Hub (student.ielts-wiz.com), recorded while you use it as a student:
    • time spent on the Hub and on each page, counted only while the Hub is on your screen and you are using it (not while it sits in a background tab or you are away);
    • study sessions: when they start and end, and your device type, screen size and browser language;
    • when you open, start and submit each homework assignment;
    • in tests, how long you spend on each question and how many times you change your answer;
    • which buttons, links and tools you use (we record the button's name, never what you type);
    • errors you run into, such as a page or a save that failed.
    We do not record your keystrokes, your screen, your location or your activity on other websites.
  • Chat status: when you were last active in the Student Hub and how far you have read in each chat. People you share a chat with see this as "online" or "last seen", and as read ticks on their messages.
  • Usage analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.
  • Browser storage: we keep your progress and settings (such as light or dark theme) in your browser's local storage so the Service works offline and loads quickly. Our authentication provider stores your sign-in session there too.

2. How we use your information

  • to create and secure your account and keep you signed in;
  • to save your practice results and sync them across your devices;
  • to score your tests and, when you ask for it, give AI feedback on your Writing;
  • to let teachers you study with set homework and see your results;
  • to show your teachers how you study, such as your time on the Student Hub, homework progress and the questions you find difficult, so they can support you;
  • to find and fix errors in the Service;
  • to send you service emails, such as homework notices and important account updates;
  • to understand which features are used so we can improve the Service;
  • to prevent abuse and keep the Service secure.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. Google user data

IELTS Wiz's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the name, email address, profile picture and account identifier we receive from Google only to create and identify your IELTS Wiz account and show your name and picture inside the Service. We do not share this data with anyone except the service providers listed below, who process it only to run the Service.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and practice data within 30 days, except where we must keep something to meet a legal obligation. Detailed study activity records (sessions, time per page and question, button use and errors) are deleted automatically after 12 months; daily totals of your time on the Student Hub are kept with your progress data. Chat messages are kept until the sender or the chat's teacher deletes them, the chat is deleted, or your account is deleted; a deleted message's text and files are removed straight away.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can see and edit your profile in the Service, or ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: email hello@ielts-wiz.com from the address on your account and we will delete your account and data.
  • Revoke Google access: you can remove IELTS Wiz at any time from your Google account connections.
  • Emails: email us to stop non-essential emails at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your data protection authority. We rely on performing our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in running and improving the Service, as the legal bases for processing.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Database access is restricted by row-level security so each user can read only their own data (and a teacher only their own classes). No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

The Service is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under the age of digital consent where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission to use it. If you believe a child has given us data without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in Japan and the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If a change is significant we will tell you by email or in the Service.
